MANGALORE REFINERY AND PETROCHEMICALS LIMITED
CENTRIFUGAL PUMP FIELD INSPECTION PROCEDURE
Document IP-7 | Revision 3 | Effective 15-06-2025

1. FREQUENCY
1.1 Critical service pumps shall be inspected monthly. General service pumps
shall be inspected quarterly. Spared pumps shall be inspected on changeover.

2. PRE-INSPECTION CHECKS
2.1 Confirm the pump is running at its normal operating point. Readings taken
at minimum flow or during recirculation are not valid for trending.
2.2 Record suction and discharge pressure, motor current and process throughput
alongside every vibration reading.

3. MEASUREMENT POINTS
3.1 Pump drive-end bearing housing, pump non-drive-end bearing housing, motor
drive-end and motor non-drive-end. Four points minimum per machine.

4. RECORDING
4.1 Each reading shall be logged against the equipment tag with date, time,
analyser serial number and the technician identification.
4.2 Readings shall be entered in the condition-monitoring register within
24 hours of measurement.

5. TREND EVALUATION
5.1 Compare against the previous three readings for the same point and
direction. An isolated high reading shall be re-measured before a work order is
raised.
5.2 Where a re-measurement confirms the value, the acceptance criteria in SOP-4
clause 4.2 apply.

6. SEAL AND LUBRICATION
6.1 Observe the seal area for visible leakage and record drops per minute.
6.2 Confirm oil level is between the marks on the constant-level oiler and that
the oil is free of visible water or metal.
