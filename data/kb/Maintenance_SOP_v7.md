MANGALORE REFINERY AND PETROCHEMICALS LIMITED
ROTATING EQUIPMENT MAINTENANCE STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Document SOP-4 | Revision 7 | Effective 01-04-2026 | Supersedes Revision 6

1. PURPOSE
This procedure defines condition-monitoring acceptance criteria and the
consequent maintenance actions for rotating equipment in CDU, VDU and allied
units. It applies to all centrifugal pumps, blowers and compressors under the
custody of the Rotating Equipment section.

2. SCOPE
Applicable to equipment tagged P-, K-, and C- series operating above 1500 rpm
and rated 15 kW or higher. Reciprocating machines are covered by SOP-9.

3. REFERENCES
3.1 ISO 10816-3 Mechanical vibration - Evaluation of machine vibration by
    measurements on non-rotating parts.
3.2 API 610 Centrifugal Pumps for Petroleum, Petrochemical and Natural Gas
    Industries, 11th edition.
3.3 MRPL-QA-12 Calibration of portable vibration analysers.

4. VIBRATION ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

4.1 Measurement shall be taken at the drive-end and non-drive-end bearing
housings in horizontal, vertical and axial directions. Readings shall be
recorded as RMS velocity in mm/s over the 10 Hz to 1000 Hz band. The analyser
shall hold a valid calibration certificate per MRPL-QA-12.

4.2 For Group 1 machines (rated above 15 kW, rigid foundation) the following
zone boundaries apply to overall RMS velocity:

| Zone | Range (mm/s RMS) | Condition | Required action |
|------|------------------|-----------|-----------------|
| A | up to 2.8 | Newly commissioned | None |
| B | 2.8 to 4.5 | Acceptable for long-term operation | Routine monitoring |
| C | 4.5 to 7.1 | Unsatisfactory for continuous operation | Plan corrective action; increase monitoring to weekly |
| D | above 7.1 | Damage may occur | Restrict operation; corrective action before next run window |

4.3 A reading in Zone C requires a work order to be raised within seven days.
A reading in Zone D requires notification to the Section Head on the same day
and shall not be cleared for continuous operation without written concurrence.

4.4 An increase exceeding 25 percent over the previous recorded reading shall be
treated as a significant trend change irrespective of the absolute zone, and
shall trigger a bearing condition assessment.

5. BEARING TEMPERATURE

5.1 Bearing housing temperature shall not exceed 85 degrees Celsius under normal
operation, measured at the housing surface adjacent to the outer race.

5.2 A rise of more than 15 degrees Celsius over the established baseline at
comparable load and ambient shall be investigated even when below the 85 degree
limit.

6. MECHANICAL SEAL

6.1 Visible seal leakage exceeding 5 drops per minute is not acceptable for
hydrocarbon service and requires seal replacement at the next opportunity.

6.2 Any leakage in light-ends or hydrogen service requires immediate isolation.

7. APPROVAL ROUTING

7.1 Recommendations arising from condition monitoring shall be compiled into an
approval note carrying: equipment tag, observed values, applicable clause of
this SOP, deviation, recommended action and target date.

7.2 Notes for Zone C findings are approved by the Section Head. Notes involving
Zone D findings, or any recommendation requiring a unit shutdown, additionally
require Head of Maintenance endorsement.
